# Panache Lashes — Migration Design Ground Truth

## Reference

The public GitHub repository at `https://github.com/Rakiafoster12/Panache` is the **ground-truth implementation** for this migration. The goal is not to redesign the business from scratch; it is to preserve the Codex-built site's established responsive editorial experience while adapting it cleanly to Manus hosting and strengthening technical and local SEO.

## Chosen Design Philosophy

**Private-studio editorial luxury:** a calm, high-touch beauty experience that feels more like a refined appointment journal than a generic salon template. The interface should communicate privacy, craftsmanship, preparation, and trust.

## Preservation Rules

The original information architecture, service guidance, Square appointment flow, accessibility content, preparation and aftercare guidance, business details, and visual identity should be preserved unless a change is necessary for deployment compatibility, accessibility, performance, security, or search visibility. Existing business photos and factual content take priority over invented material. No fabricated ratings, testimonials, or customer reviews may be introduced.

## Visual System

The existing repository's typography, palette, spacing, imagery, and motion are authoritative. Any required adaptation should retain an editorial hierarchy, generous whitespace, restrained surfaces, and asymmetric composition rather than introducing a generic centered template. The most prominent imagery should feel intimate, polished, realistic, and specific to a private lash studio in Troy, Michigan.

## Brand Essence

Panache Lashes is a private Troy, Michigan lash studio for clients seeking polished, customized eyelash extensions in a calm, prepared, one-to-one setting. Brand personality: **refined, attentive, assured**.

## Brand Voice

Headlines should be elegant and direct; calls to action should make the next step unmistakable; microcopy should be reassuring and practical. Avoid inflated promises and generic beauty clichés.

Example lines:

> Lashes designed around your features, routine, and comfort.

> Review services, prepare with confidence, then reserve your appointment through Square.

## Interaction and Motion

Interactions should feel quiet and responsive. Hover and pressed states should be immediate, page entrances subtle, and non-essential movement disabled for visitors who prefer reduced motion. Booking actions must remain visually dominant and should always lead clearly to the official Square scheduler.

## SEO and Accessibility Contract

The migration must provide crawlable page titles and descriptions, semantic heading order, descriptive links and image alternatives, canonical URLs, social metadata, local-business structured data, robots and sitemap files, fast loading, mobile usability, and clear business/location signals for Troy and nearby Oakland County searches. All factual business data must come from the repository or be confirmed by Rakia.

## Logo and Signature Color

The existing repository's logo/wordmark remains authoritative. If a supplemental symbol is needed for favicon or social use, it should be a simple lash-inspired graphic mark without generated text. The existing signature brand color will be preserved after the source styles are inspected.
